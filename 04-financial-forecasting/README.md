# Financial Forecasting and Investment Assessment

Built a 12-month revenue model to assess a proposed $130,000 investment in the Grad Careers portal.

## Deliverables

- Excel forecasting model
- Base, best and worst scenarios
- Sensitivity analysis
- Written investment recommendation

## Key findings

- Base scenario: $1,200,000 annual revenue.
- Best scenario: $1,344,000 annual revenue.
- Worst scenario: $1,140,000 annual revenue.
- The best scenario generates $144,000 in additional revenue, leaving $14,000 after the initial investment, before additional delivery and ongoing costs.

## Recommendation

Defer full investment approval until the assumed benefits, completion improvement and full costs are validated.

## Assumptions and limitations

The model assumes each percentage-point change in completion produces a 1% change in annual contract volume relative to baseline. This relationship is unverified.

Revenue less the initial investment is not profit. The analysis uses illustrative figures and excludes additional delivery and ongoing portal costs.
