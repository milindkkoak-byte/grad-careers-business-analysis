## Grad Careers Business Analysis Portfolio

Five connected projects exploring how an employment services provider could improve participant processes, monitor program performance and make evidence-based investment decisions.

Completed by **Milind Koak** as part of a business analysis practice project pack. This portfolio presents a simulated case study using illustrative data. Recommendations are proposed changes, not implemented business results.

## Business problem

The case describes spreadsheet-based referrals, manual cohort scheduling, paper attendance records, limited outcome visibility and delayed partner reporting.

The analysis explores how digital processes and better reporting could support participant completion and more informed decisions.

## Projects

| Project | Work completed | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| Process analysis and requirements | Mapped current and proposed processes, identified gaps and documented functional and non-functional requirements. | Process diagrams and requirements document |
| Power BI reporting | Analysed sample program data and presented completion, participant outcomes and partner performance. | Power BI dashboard, PDF preview, source workbook and insights report |
| User stories and prioritisation | Translated proposed capabilities into a Jira backlog, mapped the participant journey and prioritised release scope using MoSCoW. | Jira export, story map and prioritisation rationale |
| Financial forecasting | Modelled revenue scenarios and sensitivity to completion rates and contract values to assess a proposed portal investment. | Excel forecasting model and investment recommendation |
| Mentor pilot evaluation | Compared completion and recorded outcomes between control and mentor groups, assessed statistical evidence and calculated cost-effectiveness. | Excel evaluation and stakeholder presentation |

## Tools and techniques

Power BI, Excel, Jira, process mapping, gap analysis, requirements documentation, user stories, acceptance criteria, MoSCoW prioritisation, scenario modelling and statistical evaluation.

## Interpretation and limitations

- The datasets are illustrative and do not represent actual organisational performance.
- Forecast results depend on assumptions, including an unverified relationship between completion and contract volume.
- Higher observed results in the mentor group do not establish causation; assignment and baseline comparability require confirmation.
- Proposed process improvements have not been implemented or measured.
