# Mentor Pilot Evaluation

Evaluated an illustrative employer mentor pilot by comparing workshop completion and recorded participant outcomes between control and mentor groups.

## Deliverables

- Excel evaluation workbook
- Stakeholder recommendation presentation

## Analysis

Compared 2,000 participants across 40 programs: 1,000 participants in each group.

| Metric | Control | Mentor |
|---|---:|---:|
| Workshop completion | 58% | 69% |
| Recorded participant outcomes | 44% | 55% |

Both measures were 11 percentage points higher in the mentor group.

At an assumed total mentoring cost of AUD80,000, the cost per additional completion or recorded outcome observed was approximately AUD727.

These measures may overlap and must not be added together as distinct beneficiaries.

## Recommendation

Approve a limited, evaluated extension, subject to an approved budget and agreed success criteria. Defer full rollout.

## Limitations

Assignment method and baseline comparability are unconfirmed. Observed differences and statistical significance do not establish causation.

Cost-effectiveness is not financial ROI. Full delivery costs and the definition and measurement window for participant outcomes require confirmation.
